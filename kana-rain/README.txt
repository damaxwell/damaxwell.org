Kana Rain — upload instructions

1. Upload this whole folder to your site, e.g. as /kana-rain/ (any folder name works; all paths are relative).
2. Your site must be served over HTTPS for the home-screen app and offline mode to work.
3. Visit https://yoursite/kana-rain/ on your phone.
   iPhone: Share button > Add to Home Screen.
   Android (Chrome): menu > Install app / Add to Home screen.

Updating later: replace index.html, then change VERSION in sw.js (e.g. kana-rain-v2).
Phones pick up the new version the next time the app is opened (sometimes it takes two launches).

Note: progress is saved on each device, in that browser. On iPhone the home-screen app keeps
its own progress, separate from Safari.
