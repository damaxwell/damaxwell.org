\problem Let $X$ be the the set $\{(x,\sin(\pi/x))\in \Reals^2: 0<x\le 1\Ints\}$
and let $X^*=X\cup (0,1)$.  On this assignment and the next you will show
that $X^*$ is not path connected.

We'll denote
$(0,1)=*$.  Consider a path $\gamma:[0,1]\to X^*$ with $\gamma(0)=*$ 
The goal is to show that $\gamma$ is constant and hence there 
is no path from $*$ to any other point in $X^*$.

Here are the first few steps.

\begin{subproblems}
\item Let $A = \gamma^{-1}(\{*\})$. Show that if $A$ is both open and 
closed in $[0,1]$ then $\gamma(t)=*$ for all $t\in [0,1]$.
\item Show that $A$ is closed. (Hence to complete the proof we'll also show $A$ is open.)
\item Let $B= X^* \cap (\Reals\times (0,\infty))$. Make a sketch of $B$ and show that $B$ 
is open in $X^*$.
\item Let $C=\pi(B)$
where $\pi$ is projection onto the $x$-axis.  Write down 
the set $C$ explicitly as a disjoint union of open intervals along 
with the single point $0$.
\end{subproblems}
